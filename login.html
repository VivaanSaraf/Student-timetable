How this works: your password is run through PBKDF2 (150,000 rounds, SHA-256, with a random salt) and only that hash is saved — the password itself is never stored. Everything lives in this browser, so there's no server and no database. That means someone with access to this device and its developer tools could still get past it, and your account won't follow you to another device or browser. It's a school project, so please don't reuse a real password.
